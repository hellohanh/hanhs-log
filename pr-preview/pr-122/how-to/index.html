How to use

Hanh's Log, a field guide

Plan trips with the people you travel with, keep the meals worth having again, and keep the places worth finding again. All three logs share one sign-in and one map.

WanderlogYour trips: a map of pins and a day-by-day itinerary, shared with everyone on the trip.
SavorlogThe Munch List: eateries by cuisine and main dish, with MICHELIN, Vibe and everyone's WTG / VIS.
RoaminglogThe Spot List: sights, stays, shops, airports and stations, shown on every trip's map.

Chapter 1

Getting started

Open hellohanh.github.io/hanhs-log and scroll: the paper plane flies over the picture and lands, then the three logs appear under "Where to?".

Sign in and pick a log

  1. Tap Sign in and enter your email. Open the link Hanh's Log emails you.
  2. Pick Wanderlog, Savorlog or Roaminglog from the cards, or from the header at any time.
  3. The moon / sun button in the header switches between dark and light mode. Until you pick, it follows your phone or computer.

Add it to your home screen. In your phone's browser menu, choose Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app, and works with no signal too (chapter 9).

Chapter 2

Your trips

Wanderlog opens on Your trips: upcoming trips first, soonest at the top, and past trips faded underneath.

Find a trip

  • Each card shows the dates, the cities and country, how many pins and people.
  • You own this or Shared with you tells you whose trip it is.
  • ✎ edits a trip without opening it. The bin deletes it, after asking (owners only).

Start a new trip

  1. Tap New trip.
  2. Give it a name, the country and the primary city. Add up to two more cities if you like.
  3. Add the dates, or leave them empty for a trip that's still an idea.
  4. Tap Create trip. The map opens on the primary city.
Chapter 3

Places on a trip

A trip is a map with the Places list on the left. The list is a tree: category, then type, then the places. Every level starts folded up; tap a row to open it.

Read the map

  • Each pin carries its category's icon and colour. The dot on it is the latest status: WTG want to go, or a green, yellow or red mark for visited.
  • Pins that crowd together merge into a red numbered bubble. Tap it to zoom in and split it.
  • The eye on each row picks what shows when Show all pins is off, for example just your hotel and the bánh mì places.
  • The MICHELIN chip shows only MICHELIN places and hides the rest.

Every trip shows the whole Munch List and Spot List. Nom-Nom holds the eateries, and the other categories hold the spots. The list follows the map, so pan to the next city and it fills in.

Open a place

  • Tap a pin, or a place in the list, to open its popup: the name, a link to Google Maps and the note.
  • Tap the note to change it right there.
  • ✎ opens the edit panel, and the bin removes the place after asking.
  • On a computer, double-click a place in the list to go straight to its edit panel.

Add a place

  1. Type in Search Google to add a place and tap Search. Places nearest the map's centre come first.
  2. Pick the result. A place you already have says "Already added · open it".
  3. Check the English title and Local title. If Google spells it differently (accents included), a suggestion appears under the boxes; tap Use it to take Google's spelling.
  4. Pick the Category and, if you like, a group and type.
  5. Set your status: WTG, or VIS with ✓ revisit, – 2nd chance or ✗ don't go back, plus a rating in half stars.
  6. Tap Add pin.

Eateries go to the Munch List. Picking Nom-Nom opens the eatery card instead (chapter 6). Every other category saves to the Spot List, so it shows on every trip.

Chapter 4

The itinerary

Open it from the Itinerary tab on the right edge of the map. There's a tab for every trip date, plus extra days if you add them.

Plan a day

  • Pick a day's tab. The timeline shows that day's places, activities and travel as time blocks.
  • Drag a block's middle to move it, and its top or bottom edge to change the times (5-minute steps).
  • The dashed line between two blocks is a connector. Tap it to say how you get there: walk, drive, transit or bike.
  • Day route draws the day's places in order on the map.
  • ↔ makes the panel wider; ✕ closes it.

Add something at a time

  1. Click the timeline at the time you want. It snaps to the quarter hour.
  2. Choose Activity (free text), Travel (a flight, train, bus or your own transport) or Place (any pin or eatery).
  3. Add a Note if you like. It shows on the block, and in its hover text when the block is short.
  4. Tap Add.

From the Places list: with a day open, the + on a place's row adds it to that day. You can also drag a place from the list onto the timeline.

Chapter 5

Sharing a trip

Everyone on a trip can see and edit it. They're asked for a first name once, and nobody sees anyone's email.

Invite people to one trip

  1. On the trip, tap Share & people.
  2. Copy the invite link and send it. Anyone with it can join, no email needed.
  3. Owners can Remove someone, or Reset link so old links stop working.

One link for several trips

  1. On Your trips, tap Invite to several trips.
  2. Tick the trips you own that should be on the link.
  3. Tap Make one link and copy it.
  4. People sign in, see the list and tap Join all. Trips they're already on are skipped.

Stop a link: Several-trips links lists your links, each with Copy and Stop this link.

Copy pins from another trip

Going back to the same city? On the new trip tap Copy pins, pick the other trip, and its own pins (with everyone's WTG / VIS) are copied over. Places already on the trip are skipped. Eateries and spots don't need copying; they're on every trip already.

Chapter 6

Munch List

Savorlog's catalogue of eateries, filed by cuisine and main dish. It belongs to no trip: the family keeps one list, and every trip shows it.

Find eateries

  • Type a city or country in City/Country and pick it. The map glides there.
  • The list shows what's on the map. Pan to the next city and it fills in.
  • Open a cuisine, then a dish. Tap an eatery for its popup, or double-click it to edit.
  • Family (top right) holds your family's invite link and members.

Add or edit an eatery

  1. Search Google in the second box and pick the place, or double-click one you have.
  2. Pick the Cuisine, then the Main dish. The menu is A–Z; + Add a new dish… at the top adds one to that cuisine for everyone.
  3. Set the Price ($ to $$$$).
  4. MICHELIN Guide: None, Selected or Bib on the first line; ★, ★★ or ★★★ on the second. Add the year. Bib places get the smiling-face chip on their pin.

Vibe, photos and your status

  • Vibe: tick any that fit. Dine in, Fine dining, Street food, Food truck, Outdoor seating, Community seating, Food court / hawker, Counter / bar seating and Takeaway / to-go. Ticked vibes show under the name in the popup.
  • You: WTG, or VIS with a verdict and a rating.
  • Photos: up to 3. The first is the cover; with none, a Google photo is used.
  • Remove… sends the eatery to the owner's pending-deletion list. Only the owner removes it for good.
Chapter 7

Spot List

Roaminglog's catalogue of everything that isn't an eatery: hotels, sights, shops, airports and stations. Like the Munch List it belongs to no trip, and every trip's map shows it.

Use the Spot List

  • It works like the Munch List: City/Country moves the map, the list follows the map, and Google search adds a spot.
  • Tap a row or pin for the popup; double-click a row to edit.
  • A spot you add on a trip lands here too, and the same spot on two trips is one spot, with one set of notes and everyone's WTG / VIS.
  • Removing a spot sends it to the owner's pending-deletion list and takes it off every trip.
Chapter 8

On your phone

On a phone the map fills the screen and the lists slide up as sheets. Everything from the chapters above is there, arranged for one hand.

  • Sheets: swipe a sheet's handle up for half, again for full, and back down to close it to just the handle.
  • Bottom bar: Places and Itinerary swap the sheets. Tapping the open one drops it.
  • ⋯ on a trip holds Edit trip, Share & people and Map look. ☰ holds the three logs, dark / light mode and Sign out.
  • Back gesture: steps back one layer at a time.
Chapter 9

No signal

Whatever you've opened is kept on your phone or computer. With no signal you can still read it, and change it; your changes send themselves when the signal comes back.

Before you go

  1. While you have signal, open each trip you'll need, plus the Munch List and the Spot List.
  2. In the Google Maps app, download the area as an offline map. Hanh's Log's own map needs a connection.

With no signal

  • A dark line says Offline · showing what was saved Tue 9:42.
  • Make changes as usual. Each one is kept on the device, marked waiting to send with a dashed outline, and a yellow line counts them.
  • When the signal returns they send in the order you made them, and the lines go.

Still needs a signal: photo uploads, invite links, Copy pins, and sending an eatery or spot to the removal list.

When someone else changed it too

If Huong changed the same thing while you were offline, sending pauses and you pick which version stays.

  • Keep mine sends yours. Keep Huong's leaves hers.
  • Only what you actually changed is sent, so if Huong changed something else on the same item, both changes stay.
  • If the item was removed meanwhile, a red line tells you, with Dismiss.
